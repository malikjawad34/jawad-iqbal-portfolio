## Visual system

Thesis: an editorial engineering portfolio with a calm, dark opening and clear light-background case studies. Typography, spacing, and diagrams carry the design.

- Ink #101419; action blue #315CEC; hero blue #7B9BFF; white paper; light slate section background.
- Inter variable, hosted locally with next/font; Courier New for small metadata.
- Large fluid headlines, narrow prose, a 1328px maximum grid, generous section rhythm.
- Fine rules and small-radius controls; no ornamental glass or gradients.
- Diagrams are functional summaries of documented technology layers, not product screenshots or exact infrastructure topology.
- Mobile uses a compact three-column engineering overview, simplified proof strip, stacked case studies, and accessible disclosure navigation.
- Motion: short hover transitions, restrained button movement, native anchor scrolling. Reduced-motion disables these transitions and smooth scrolling. Content is never hidden waiting for animation.
